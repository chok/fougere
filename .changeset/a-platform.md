What a process carries is `platform:`: the extensions it mounts, imported from their package — `platform: [log()]`. A process of `processes:` states its own list or carries the config's, exactly the list; to add to the default, spread it. An extension written under `fronds:` (`'@fougere/log': {}`) now refuses the boot, naming the `platform:` line to write.
