An axis is one file, and the conversion a card needs is stated only by the axis that has one.

`LifecycleRules`, `LifecycleAxis` and `Lifecycle` were one subject in three files; so were the
role and boundary trios. `Axis.describe` and `Axis.reconstruct` are optional: `role` states them
because its card carries a name where its declaration carries a function, and the other two stop
writing `(value) => value` twice.
