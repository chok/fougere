A name says what it does. `FrondsStated`, `FrondStated` and `StatedFrond` are `FrondEntries`, `FrondEntry`
and `KeyedFrondEntry`; `StatedOperation` is `StatementOperation`; `Carry` is `LineBuffer` (`lineBuffer` on
`LoggerOptions`); `Hosting` is `RowHosts`; a seam is a host port (`HOST_PORTS`); `statedModules` is
`configModules`; `BUILT` is `BUILD_DIRECTORY`. On the wire, `invocation.crossed` is `fromAnotherProcess`
and `OperationContext.crosses` is `toAnotherProcess` — both ends move together. `FrondDescriptor.brought`
is `fromExtension`, `CallRecord.verdict` is `status`, `state.forget(name)` is `state.delete(name)`, and a
binding source states `bind(param)` where it stated `claim(param)`.
