---
title: "A frond in another language"
description: "A Rust or Python process answering the same calls."
kind: "extending"
---

A frond does not have to be TypeScript. A process in Rust, Python or anything else joins the app if it
honours three things:

1. **the wire** — `POST /_fougere/call`, JSON-RPC 2.0, `method = "address.op"` — see
   [the wire format](/docs/extending/card-and-wire#the-wire-format);
2. **the card** — `rpc.discover` answers its [card](/docs/extending/card-and-wire), `x-fougere-version: 1`;
3. **the errors** — a whole `FougereError` in `error.data`, its `code` one of the
   [error codes](/reference/errors); the integer `-32000` stays JSON-RPC's.

Your app then runs `fougere sync` against it, rebuilds its entities from the card, and checks data
locally with the same rules as for any frond.

`demos/rust-frond/` is one, in Rust — one entity, three operations, no `entity({...})` anywhere.
