REST, GraphQL, the call envelope and the terminal are all doors onto the same operations. A surface of your own — RSS, a webhook — is one more door, written the same way.

## Every door runs one cycle

REST, GraphQL, the call envelope and the terminal are four doors over one cycle, `app.answer(door,
received)`: a door package brings `decode`, `run` and `encode` in its own terms, and core runs them —
entering the request so every call it makes says which one it answers, announcing `received`,
`decoded`, `encoded` and `failed` beside the calls to whoever observes the app, keeping the
[caller's state](/docs/access/state), and making a refusal public by one rule before the door
says it. Core names no door: the cycle is the same for a door it has never heard of.

## Writing your own

A custom surface such as RSS, webhook, or CLI follows the same sequence. The
[invoke](/docs/serve/pages) page includes a complete RSS example.
